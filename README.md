## Datasets
1. **_ATT_**

AT&T Database of Faces. Downloaded from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kasikrit/att-database-of-faces

2. **_stock_XXXX_YYYY.csv_**

Daily adjusted closing prices for given years (XXXX–YYYY) based on CRSP (Center for Research in Security Prices) standards.
Files are generated in the **HSI** folder.

3. **_AAL.csv_**, **_FB.csv_**, **_GS.csv_**, **_MS.csv_**

Daily price data including HLOC, Adjusted Close, and Volume from 03/15/2016 to 03/12/2021.

4. **_Bitstamp_BTCUSD_2018_minute.csv_**

Minute-level Bitcoin price data (HLOC and Volume). Downloaded from https://www.CryptoDataDownload.com

5. **_Credit Card Customer Data.csv_**

Downloaded from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aryashah2k/credit-card-customer-data

6. **_Credit default.csv_**

Default of credit card clients dataset. Downloaded from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uciml/default-of-credit-card-clients-dataset

7. **_fin-ratio.csv_** and **_fin-ratio_2018.csv_**

HSI financial ratios for 2002 and 2018, respectively.

8. **_fin-ratio_cleansed.csv_**

Cleansed version of the 2002 HSI financial ratios dataset, with outliers removed using Mahalanobis distance.

9. **_FTSE.csv_**, **_HSI.csv_**, **_SPX.csv_**

Daily closing prices from 4/1/1984 to 27/4/2021.

10. **_Medicalpremium.csv_**

Medical insurance premium prediction dataset. Downloaded from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tejashvi14/medical-insurance-premium-prediction

11. **_Mroz.csv_**

1976 Panel Study of Income Dynamics dataset. Downloaded from Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/utkarshx27/labor-supply-data

12. **_S&P500_Open_hourly_YYYY.csv_**

Hourly adjusted opening prices for all S&P 500 stocks in year YYYY.

13. **_SPX_Open_hourly_YYYY.csv_**

Hourly opening prices for the S&P 500 index in year YYYY.

14. **_SPX_Open_2020H.csv_**

Daily opening prices for the S&P 500 index from 01/02/2020 to 06/30/2020.

15. **_us_Open_2020H.csv_**

Daily adjusted opening prices for individual S&P 500 stocks from 01/02/2020 to 06/30/2020.

16. **_us-rate.csv_**

US semi-annualized zero-coupon rates (basis points) from 1944 to 1992.

17. **_wordle.csv_**

List of selected 5-letter words (randomly shuffled). Retreived from https://github.com/tabatkins/wordle-list
